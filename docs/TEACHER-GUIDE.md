# Griffin Grader: one page for teachers

Attendance and rubric grading for PE, straight from your iPad. Everything you
record stays on your iPad. Nothing is ever sent anywhere.

## 1. Put it on your Home Screen first

This matters. iPadOS can clear what a Safari tab stores if a site goes unused
for a while. Opening Griffin Grader from the Home Screen keeps your classes.

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. From now on, open Griffin Grader from the Home Screen icon.

After that it works with no wifi at all.

## 2. Add a class

Tap **New class**, give it the name you use on your timetable, then add your
students one of two ways:

- **Paste names**: one student per line. `Bea Ruiz`, or `Ruiz, Bea`, or paste
  straight from a spreadsheet. If the names come out backwards, tap
  **Swap first and last**.
- **Import CSV**: pick a roster exported from PowerSchool. Check the column
  dropdowns are right and change any that are not.

Either way you get a table you can fix before saving. Tap **Remove** on a row
you do not want.

## 3. Take attendance

Tap **Start class**. Everyone begins as **Absent**, and the app opens in
attendance mode.

- Tap a student to cycle **Absent → Present → Late → Absent**.
- Or tap **Mark all present** and then tap the few who are away.
- Tap **Done** when the roll is right.

A student who arrives late: press and hold their tile for half a second. That
cycles their attendance without opening anything.

## 4. Grade

Tap a student. For each category, tap the level you saw: DV1 through EX2.

- It saves the moment you tap. A bar appears at the bottom with **Undo** if you
  hit the wrong one.
- A comment box opens under the level you just tapped. Type, or tap one of the
  quick comments. It saves itself.
- You can record a category more than once in a period. Each one counts.

The number on a tile is how many entries that student has today. A **blue ring**
means they are here and you have not graded them yet.

The gold number in the top bar, **Graded 13/20**, is how many of the students
who are here have at least one entry. That is the number to watch.

Grading someone marked Absent marks them Present automatically.

## 5. Split into teams

Tap **Teams**, choose **Groups of** a number or **Number of teams**, and tap
**Make teams**. By default only students who are here get a team.

Teams are even: 22 students in groups of 4 gives you 4, 4, 4, 4, 3, 3, never a
team of 2 against teams of 4.

Switch between **Grid** and **Teams** at the bottom. Tiles still work for
grading in either view. To move someone, tap **Move students**, tap them, then
tap the team you want them in.

## 6. End the class

Tap **End class**. You get the attendance totals, the number of entries, and a
list of anyone who was here but never got graded.

## 7. Get the marks into PowerSchool

Tap **Export**. Pick **Today** (or another range), and you get a table on screen
with one row per student, their attendance, and their latest level per category.

You can type straight into PowerSchool from that table without opening a file.
If you would rather have the file, tap **Download PowerSchool sheet** and save
it to Files or send it to yourself.

**Full log** is the complete record: every entry with its time and comment.

## 8. Back up every week

This is the one piece of housekeeping. Your classes live only on this iPad.

Go to **Settings** and tap **Download backup**, then save it somewhere that is
not the iPad: Files, email it to yourself, anywhere. The app will remind you if
it has been more than a week.

To put a backup back, go to **Settings**, tap **Restore a backup**, pick the
file, check the counts it shows you, and confirm.

**If you delete Griffin Grader from your Home Screen, its data goes with it.**
A backup is the only way back.

## Small things worth knowing

- Two students with the same first name show as "Alex Mo." and "Alex Ma." so you
  can tell them apart.
- Removing a student keeps their history. You can put them back from the class
  page.
- Changing a rubric does not change marks you already recorded.
- The screen stays awake while a class is open.
- When a new version is ready you get a banner. It never reloads during a class.
